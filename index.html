How this works — and MT5

MT5 on your phone has no built-in market replay. Paid replay tools on MQL5 only run on MT5 Desktop (Windows). MT5 Manager access is broker-only — MetaQuotes licences it to the company running the MT5 server, a retail trader cannot get it, and replay doesn't need it anyway: replay is a client-side simulation.

This app is the phone solution: it loads real historical candles (bundled in the repo, refreshed from Kraken for crypto and Yahoo Finance for gold/FX), hides everything after your start point, and replays them tick-by-tick while you trade with simulated money. No broker, no login, no Manager access. Add it to your Home Screen from your browser menu and it works like an app, offline.

Full explanation + MT5 desktop options: MT5-EXPLAINED.md in the repo.
